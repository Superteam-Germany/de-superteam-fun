Why build with Superteam Germany?

We provide resources, mentorship and connect you with like-minded people to support you throughout the hackathon and beyond.

Boost Your Odds: Pressure-test your idea early and improve the clarity of your submission.

Expert Support: Workshops, pitch feedback and introductions to experienced builders and founders.

Massive Exposure: Showcase your work to Germany’s community and the wider Superteam network.

Prizes & Beyond: Build toward the hackathon while creating something strong enough to continue afterwards.

A Network That Lasts: Connect with potential teammates, partners, mentors and early users.
