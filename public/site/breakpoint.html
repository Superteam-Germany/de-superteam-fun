Good to know

Before
you go.

A few answers to help you take the next step.

What is Breakpoint?

Breakpoint is Solana’s annual conference, bringing together builders, founders, and the wider Solana community. It’s a place to discover what’s being built, exchange ideas, and meet people from across the ecosystem. Explore the official Breakpoint website.

Do I need to be a Superteam member?

No. You’re welcome to connect with our community whether you’re an official member, have joined an event before, or are meeting us for the first time.

Is this just for developers?

Founders, developers, designers, creators, operators, and curious newcomers are welcome. If you’re interested in Solana and want to meet the people building it, come say hello.

How do I get a Breakpoint ticket?

Our Breakpoint ticket link applies the 25% community discount automatically. You can also enter SUPERTEAM-6NQP-BP26 at checkout. Joining our Telegram connects you with the community; it doesn’t register you for the conference.

Where can I find the programme and event details?

The official Breakpoint website has the programme, speakers, ticket options, and venue information. Breakpoint 2026 takes place 15–17 November at Olympia in London.
